How it works

  • 1
    Register once: Click the button below to complete the Google Form details so we can handle your parcels.
  • 2
    Order as usual: Choose the res gate as your delivery address, or tell us where it must be collected.
  • 3
    Send us your code: When the courier arrives, share your delivery code or OTP on WhatsApp so we can sign for it.
  • 4
    We deliver to your door: Parcels are delivered in set time slots. You confirm receipt with a PIN or a WhatsApp reply.
