How it works

  • 1
    Register once: Send your name, room number and phone, and agree that we can receive and store your parcels.
  • 2
    Order as usual: Choose the res gate as your delivery address, or tell us where it must be collected.
  • 3
    Send us your code: When the courier arrives, share your delivery code or OTP on WhatsApp so we can sign for it.
  • 4
    We deliver to your door: Parcels are delivered in set time slots. You confirm receipt with a PIN or a WhatsApp reply.

Keeping your parcels safe

  • Every parcel is logged and photographed when it arrives and when it leaves us.
  • Parcels are tagged with your name and room and kept locked away.
  • Gate security may check parcels before they enter. You agree to this when you register.
  • We only hand over to you, using your PIN or confirmation.
